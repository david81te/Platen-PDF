Left out on purpose

  • Certificate-based signing, validation and e-signature sending — the requirement was to paste a signature image and lock it, not to issue verifiable signatures.
  • Accessibility tagging and cloud collaboration — ruled out at the start.
  • Draw-a-signature and type-a-signature — an image covers the need.

Where Acrobat still earns its keep

  • A counterparty needs a verifiable digital signature, or you must check one you have been sent.
  • Legal discovery work needing Bates numbering, or documents that must meet accessibility rules.
  • Commercial print or PDF/A archiving.
  • Editing on a Mac, iPad or phone, or reviewing with people outside the firm.
